# Code of Conduct (org-wide default)

Every repository in the AEP organization follows the **Contributor Covenant
v2.1**; the canonical copy lives in the standard repository:
[CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](https://github.com/agenteventprotocol/agent-event-protocol/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported to the maintainers per the governance process
([GOVERNANCE.md §7](https://github.com/agenteventprotocol/agent-event-protocol/blob/main/GOVERNANCE.md)).
All complaints are reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly; maintainers
respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.
